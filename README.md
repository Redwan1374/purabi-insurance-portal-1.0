# Purabi General Insurance Portal

A modern, responsive, and user-friendly insurance website designed for **Purabi General Insurance Company Limited (PGICL)**.

The project presents a complete digital insurance experience with dedicated pages for the homepage, health insurance, and company information. It focuses on clean UI, responsive layouts, intuitive navigation, insurance plan discovery, claim tracking, and customer engagement.

## 📌 Project Overview

The Purabi General Insurance Portal is a frontend web application created to provide a professional digital presence for an insurance company.

The website includes:

- Insurance service discovery
- Health insurance plan selection
- Insurance quotation interface
- Claim tracking section
- Company information
- Trusted partners and clients
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Insurance working process
- News & events
- Mobile application promotion
- Payment channel information
- Responsive navigation and footer

The interface follows a modern corporate insurance design with a consistent visual identity, clear calls-to-action, and responsive layouts.

The website follows a professional corporate insurance aesthetic.
